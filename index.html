01 · Points are facts

Every number has a source.

Each value Clucy applies traces to an approved rule: its source, edition and page. Where no rule covers it, Clucy labels it an assumption and shows it as one.

02 · Lines are rules

Design is data before it is geometry.

Clucy plans as a checked model first: rooms, walls and doors as data, validated against the rules. Only then does deterministic code build anything in Rhino or Revit.

03 · Space is the result

It reads a plan the way you walk it.

The spatial layer measures how a plan works, on your own model.

  1. RoomsEvery room as a measured field.
  2. AdjacencyWhich rooms connect, and through which doors.
  3. CirculationThe routes people take from the entrance.
  4. Daylight and viewWhere light falls, and what you see from where you stand.

04 · The gate

Nothing moves without you.

Clucy shows the plan, every value with its source and every assumption it made. Nothing touches your Rhino or Revit file until you approve, and each approved change is one undo step.
