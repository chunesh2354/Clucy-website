# Brand on the site

Source: Clucy brand rules v1.0. Where the visual reference and the brand disagree, the brand wins.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Ink | `#15140F` | Main ground; panels on ink |
| Paper | `#F7F4EC` | Ground for the plan chapters (rules, space) |
| Paper on ink | `#F5F2EA` | Points, text and walls on ink |
| Ultramarine | `#2B3BD9` | Dot and people on paper; demo poster ground |
| Ultramarine on ink | `#8592FF` | Dot, current and people on ink |
| Amber / on ink | `#A35F00` / `#E3A13A` | Only at the gate: "waiting for you" |
| Stone / on ink | `#645F57` / `#B3ADA1` | Secondary text |
| Graphite | `#3B3830` | Secondary buttons and chips on ink |

Grounds are ink, paper and ultramarine only. There are no gradients, glows, shadows, blur or outlines anywhere: points are hard-edged discs, depth comes from dot size and three opacity steps (100, 65 and 35 %, the Contour's steps), and brightness comes from how densely points pack. The ink-to-paper change is a hard-edged wipe that opens from the dot.

## From the reference to the brand

| Reference | Site | Why |
|-----------|------|-----|
| Glowing, blurred trails | Crisp dots on discrete streamlines | No glows or gradients |
| White wave | Wall of the Room (a squircle opening east), highest at the back | The wave becomes the Room |
| Red-orange current | Ultramarine current through the east opening, 80° fan | Red means "down" in Clucy; the Contour opens east at 80° |
| Figure at ground zero | Figure on the threshold dot | Every movement starts at the dot |

## Type

Geist (400, 500, 700) and Geist Mono (400, 500), self-hosted. Headlines in Geist Bold with -4.5 % tracking end on the dot-coloured full stop, like the wordmark. Kickers, rule IDs and labels in Geist Mono caps, +8 % tracking.

## Logo

Horizontal lockup in the header. The Room is never bent, rotated or mirrored by the effect; the field only takes its shape. The closing reveal follows the brand motion spec: the dot, then the walls drawn from the opening around and back (480 ms), then the name (720 ms), settle easing, no loop.

## Pet

Tile appears at the gate (amber eye), jumps once when the build lands, and sits in the footer following the cursor with its eye. It never covers text.
