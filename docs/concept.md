# Concept

Approved 11 Nov 2026.

**Big idea.** Points are facts. Lines are rules. Space is the result. A vast field of moving points bends around one small person standing on Clucy's dot. As you scroll, the field condenses into a plan, the plan is read as space, it stops at your approval, and only then does it rise into a building.

The visual reference was a photograph-like image of light streams bending through space around a tiny figure. The site takes its feeling (vast data, one human for scale) and rebuilds it from the Clucy brand: the rising wave is the Room's wall, the current leaves through the Room's east opening, and the figure stands on the threshold dot.
